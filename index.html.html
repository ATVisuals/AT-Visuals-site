From raw files to final export

Four steps, in this order. You see the work early, while changes are still cheap.

Step 1

Brief

Share the footage, a reference video you like and the platform. We confirm length, style and deadline.

Step 2

Rough cut

We assemble the story with placeholder music and no polish, so you can judge structure first.

Step 3

Revisions

You send timestamped notes. We apply them, then add graphics, colour and the sound mix.

Step 4

Delivery

Final files in every ratio you asked for, plus the project file if you want to keep editing.
